Building a Nation Beyond Borders

The Association of Ugandan Students in India (AUSI) is the official representative body for Ugandan students pursuing higher education across India. Founded in 2023 under the patronage of the Uganda High Commission in New Delhi, AUSI bridges the gap between students and the academic, cultural, and diplomatic communities they navigate every day — ensuring no student faces the journey of studying abroad alone.

Leadership

AUSI is governed by an elected 11-member national cabinet, supported by Regional Representatives across key university cities — Rajkot, Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, Mumbai, Delhi, and beyond. Each cabinet member serves a one-year term, championing student welfare, cultural programming, institutional relationships, and academic support within their remit.

Our Mission

AUSI exists to represent, unite, and empower every Ugandan student in India — ensuring each one has the support, community, and platform needed to thrive.

  • Student Representation Advocating for students' rights, welfare, and interests with Indian universities and the Uganda High Commission.
  • Academic Development Facilitating mentorship, study resources, and pathways to academic excellence across disciplines.
  • Student Welfare Providing support for mental health, financial hardship, medical emergencies, housing, and repatriation needs.
  • Cultural Identity Celebrating Ugandan heritage through independence commemorations, cultural showcases, traditional performances, and national days.
  • Admissions & Orientation Guiding prospective and newly arrived students through applications, travel, and the transition into university life in India.
  • Community Service Giving back through organised clean-up drives, outreach programmes, and partnerships with local communities.
  • Sports & Wellness Organising football leagues, marathons, and annual inter-university games to foster fitness, teamwork, and camaraderie.
  • Institutional Partnerships Building lasting relationships with Indian universities, government bodies, and Ugandan diaspora networks.
  • Recognition & Achievement Championing student innovations, academic honours, sports achievements, and outstanding community contributions.

Student Welfare & Support

AUSI actively supports students facing financial hardship, medical emergencies, and accommodation challenges. Working alongside the Uganda High Commission, the association provides guidance on insurance claims, health access, hospital navigation, and repatriation support. Peer-support channels are maintained year-round to ensure no student is left to face the system alone. The welfare office coordinates directly with university administrations to resolve issues quickly and confidentially.

Looking Ahead

As AUSI enters its third year under President Abaho Colleb, the association is expanding its regional network, deepening partnerships with key Indian universities, and formalising a scholarship and mentorship fund for new Ugandan students. An annual National Leadership Summit and inter-university sports tournament are planned for 2027. The coming year marks a new chapter — more structured, more connected, and more impactful than ever before.
