About AUSI

The Voice of Ugandan Students — at Every University in India

The Association of Ugandan Students in India is the official representative body for Ugandans studying across Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, NCT Delhi, and beyond.

Who We Are

About AUSI

The Association of Ugandan Students in India — official, organised, and built to last.

Our Story

Two Decades of Community, One Association

Founded in 2004, the Association of Ugandan Students in India was born from a simple need — Ugandan students arriving at Indian universities needed each other. What started as informal meetups in Pune grew into a structured national body with elected leadership, five active chapters, and a presence at 25+ universities across 7 Indian states.

AUSI serves as the formal liaison between Ugandan students and the Uganda High Commission in New Delhi. We advocate for student welfare, negotiate with university administrations, and ensure every student — regardless of which campus they are on — feels represented.

Today AUSI represents over 1,240 active students and 380+ alumni worldwide. Our digital platform exists to extend that community beyond geography and institutional boundaries.
