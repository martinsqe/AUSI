About AUSI

The Voice of Ugandan Students in India — at Every University

The Association of Ugandan Students in India (AUSI) is the official representative body for Ugandans studying at universities across multiple Indian states — from Maharashtra to Telangana, Karnataka to NCT Delhi, Andhra Pradesh, and beyond.

No matter which university you attend or which state you're in, AUSI is your community. AUSI is our digital evolution — built to preserve institutional memory, bridge students across institutions, and connect everyone from first-years in Pune to final-years in Visakhapatnam.

Our Mission
Unite. Empower. Represent.

To unite Ugandan students across every university in India — providing structured community, cross-institutional support, mentorship pathways, and a direct channel to the Uganda High Commission and homeland.
